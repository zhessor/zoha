# Skill: proposals and quotations 

## Structure
1. Title: "Proposal: <what> for <client>" + date + "Prepared by <the user's business>".
2. Their situation in 2–3 lines (what they have now and what it costs them – e.g. "no mobile site, enquiries only by phone").
3. What we'll do: deliverables as a short list, each with a one-line outcome.
4. Timeline: phases with week numbers (Week 1: …).
5. Investment: a table – item | details | ₹ amount; subtotal, GST 18% on a separate line, total. Payment terms (e.g. 50% advance, 50% on launch).
6. What we need from them (logo, photos, content, access).
7. Why us: 2–3 specific points about the user's business, no fluff.
8. Next step + validity ("Valid till <date>") + contact.

## Rules
- Specific numbers, no ranges like "₹10k–50k" unless asked. Indian grouping (₹45,000; ₹1,25,000).
- Plain language a shop owner understands – no jargon (say "shows up on Google Maps", not "local SEO schema").
- One page if possible; never more than two.

## Checklist
- [ ] Prices add up; GST shown separately; total correct
- [ ] Deliverables, timeline and payment terms present
- [ ] Clear next step and validity date
