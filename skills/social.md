# Skill: social media (Instagram, LinkedIn, reels) for the user and their clients

## Rules
- First line = the hook (what shows before "… more"): a sharp claim, a number or a question. Under 125 characters.
- Write for one reader: a Mumbai shop/business owner. Concrete examples (a Thane kitchen showroom, a Bandra salon).
- One idea per post. One call to action ("DM 'SITE' for a free audit", "Link in bio").
- Instagram: short lines, white space, max 5 relevant hashtags at the end (mix of local + topic, e.g. #MumbaiBusiness #ThaneWest).
- Reels script: Hook (0–3 s) → problem → 2–3 quick points → payoff → CTA. Write the on-screen text and the spoken line separately.
- LinkedIn: no hashtag spam, a story or lesson, 900–1,300 characters.
- Hinglish only if asked; otherwise simple Indian English.

## Checklist
- [ ] Hook works on its own
- [ ] One clear CTA
- [ ] ≤ 5 hashtags, all relevant
