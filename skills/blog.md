# Skill: blog posts and articles (Markdown)

## Rules
- Title that promises something specific ("5 reasons Thane interior firms lose enquiries on mobile"), then a 2-line intro with the answer up front.
- H2 sections that each make one point; short paragraphs; a list or table where it helps scanning.
- Real, local examples and numbers (₹, Mumbai areas). No invented statistics – say "in our experience" instead of fake percentages.
- End with a clear next step (contact, WhatsApp, free audit).
- SEO: the main phrase in the title, first paragraph and one H2; a 150-character meta description at the top as `Description: …`.
- 700–1,200 words unless asked otherwise.

## Checklist
- [ ] Answer is in the first two lines
- [ ] Every section makes one clear point
- [ ] No made-up statistics
- [ ] Ends with a next step
