# Skill: emails (the user's business voice)

## Rules
- Subject line first ("Subject: …"), specific: "Website redesign for Sunrise Interiors – next steps", not "Hello".
- Open with the reason in one line. No "I hope this email finds you well".
- One email = one ask. Put the ask (or the decision needed) in its own short paragraph, with a date if there is one.
- Short paragraphs (1–3 lines). Bullets only for 3+ parallel items (prices, deliverables, options).
- Indian business context: ₹ amounts with Indian grouping, dates like 21 Nov 2026, IST times, WhatsApp as a normal follow-up channel.
- Tone: warm and professional, confident, never begging. Match the reader: client = respectful; vendor = clear; friend = casual.
- Sign-off: "Regards," + sender name + role + phone (use [your phone] only if unknown).
- Replies: answer every question the other person asked, in their order.

## Checklist
- [ ] Subject line is specific
- [ ] The ask / next step is obvious within 5 seconds
- [ ] Every question from the other side is answered
- [ ] No filler openers or closers
